## Cast datetime choice variants before COALESCE to prevent implicit timezone conversion
- Affected: `Observation.effective[x]` and any Pathling ViewDefinition datetime choices that mix string-valued `dateTime`/`Period.start` aliases with a native Spark `timestamp` alias such as `instant`
- Verified: `coagulation` attempt_0002 conflicted on `charttime` for 1,543,003/1,543,003 keyed rows while every other value matched. Pathling 9.6.0 materialized `effective_datetime` and `effective_period_start` as `string` but `effective_instant` as `timestamp`; Spark 4.0.2 promoted `COALESCE(effective_datetime, effective_period_start, effective_instant)` to offset-aware `timestamp` before the outer `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` cast. At session timezone `Australia/Sydney`, a served `2125-02-27T14:00:00-05:00` became `2125-02-28 06:00:00`, whereas casting `effective_datetime` directly to `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` preserved `2125-02-27 14:00:00`. Cast each string choice to `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` before any COALESCE, and do not include an unused native-`timestamp` variant.

## An outer TIMESTAMP cast reintroduces timezone conversion after TIMESTAMP_NTZ parsing
- Affected: `Observation.effective[x]` and every offset-bearing FHIR datetime output passed from `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` to Spark `TIMESTAMP`
- Verified: `coagulation` attempt_0003 had `charttime` `differing_conflict` on 1,543,003/1,543,003 keyed full-data rows while every other column matched; its direct `TRY_CAST(effective_datetime AS TIMESTAMP_NTZ)` was correct, but final `CAST(charttime AS TIMESTAMP)` changed the output to Spark `TIMESTAMP_LTZ`. A Spark 4.0.2 probe at `Australia/Sydney` reproduced full-data samples exactly: `2134-10-10T00:04:00-04:00` parsed to NTZ `2134-10-10 00:04:00`, then the outer cast encoded `2134-10-09 13:04:00Z` (-11h), while `2183-06-05T06:30:00-04:00` encoded `2183-06-04 20:30:00Z` (-10h). Keep the outermost manifest cast as `TIMESTAMP_NTZ`; a late plain `TIMESTAMP` cast undoes the wall-clock preservation.

## Lab DST-gap charttimes are irreversibly normalized before FHIR serialization
- Affected: `Observation.effectiveDateTime` for labevents-derived Observations and `Specimen.collection.collectedDateTime` for lab specimens
- Verified: `coagulation` attempt_0004 reproduced 1,542,888/1,543,003 keyed full-data rows identically and had only 115 `differing_conflict` rows, all on `charttime` and all exactly +1 hour candidate versus oracle, with zero missing, candidate-only, or null-only rows. `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_observation_labevents.sql:15,121` casts source `labevents.charttime` through `TIMESTAMPTZ` and writes the resulting value to `effectiveDateTime`; the parallel specimen path computes `MAX(charttime)` at `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_specimen_lab.sql:9`, casts it through `TIMESTAMPTZ` at line 18, and writes it at line 58. A nonexistent spring-forward 02:xx and a genuine 03:xx therefore serialize identically as 03:xx, so neither FHIR field can recover the source wall time.
