## Patient race extension collapses admission race to four broad values
- Affected: `Patient.extension('http://hl7.org/fhir/us/core/StructureDefinition/us-core-race')` and any source `mimiciv_hosp.admissions.race` output
- Verified: `icustay_detail` attempt_0001 embedded Pathling/Spark probe over `/Users/nau025/warehouses/mimic-iv-demo/delta` found 100/100 Patient race texts but only `Black or African American`, `White`, `unknown`, and `asked but unknown`; the 140-row `mimiciv_derived.icustay_detail` oracle had 14 detailed race values. Mapping those four texts back to their obvious source categories agreed on 122/140 ICU-stay rows, so the detailed admission race is not exactly recoverable.

## Correction: Patient race is latest-admission and many-to-one OMB mapped, not limited to four texts
- Affected: `Patient.extension('http://hl7.org/fhir/us/core/StructureDefinition/us-core-race')`, `Patient.extension('http://hl7.org/fhir/us/core/StructureDefinition/us-core-ethnicity')`, and any source `mimiciv_hosp.admissions.race` output
- Verified: `icustay_detail` attempt_0001 full-data comparison found 13,246 `race` conflicts. The full candidate carried eight post-SQL race values, adding Asian, American Indian/Alaska Native, Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander, and `other` to the four seen in demo. Read-only full-data replay of `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_patient.sql:23-30,59,110`, `fhir_etl/map_race_omb.sql:13-45`, and `fhir_etl/map_ethnicity.sql:13-47` showed 1,766 current conflicts are recoverable by decoding both extensions to canonical broad MIMIC labels; 9,939 are same-admission detailed values lost in the many-to-one maps and 1,541 are current-admission values replaced by the ETL's latest-admission selection.

## Hospital expiry and admission death time are not serialized into FHIR
- Affected: `Encounter` (absent `admissions.hospital_expire_flag` and `admissions.deathtime`), `Patient.deceasedDateTime`, and `Encounter.hospitalization.dischargeDisposition`
- Verified: `icustay_detail` attempt_0001 full-data comparison found 187 `hospital_expire_flag` conflicts, all oracle 0 versus candidate 1. Tightening the available Patient-DOD derivation from "between admission and discharge dates" to "equals discharge date" resolves 5, but 182 false rows then have the same death-date relation as all 8,329 true rows; those 182 also span `DIED` (111), non-`DIED` (68), and null (3) discharge dispositions, each of which also occurs on true rows. `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_patient.sql:52,109` writes only the patient-level DOD date, while `fhir_encounter.sql:65-68,149-169` writes the period and discharge location but neither source death field.

## Correction: hospital expiry must be a declared typed NULL, not a DOD estimate
- Affected: `Encounter` (absent `admissions.hospital_expire_flag` and `admissions.deathtime`) and any derived `hospital_expire_flag` output
- Verified: `icustay_detail` attempt_0001 full-data diagnosis established that no exact expression over `Patient.deceasedDateTime`, `Encounter.period`, or `Encounter.hospitalization.dischargeDisposition` reproduces the source flag: the attempted DOD-window estimate caused 187 `differing_conflict` rows and even the stricter discharge-date equality leaves 182. Because `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_patient.sql:52,109` and `fhir_encounter.sql:59-71,149-169` serialize neither source field, a port must emit `CAST(NULL AS SMALLINT)` and declare the column unrepresentable rather than manufacture a contested conflict.

## ICU Encounter serializes transformed period endpoints but omits source ICU LOS
- Affected: `Encounter.period.start`, `Encounter.period.end` on the `encounter-icu` stream, and the absent `mimiciv_icu.icustays.los` value
- Verified: `icustay_detail` attempt_0002 full comparison saw 16 `icu_intime`, 3 `icu_outtime`, and 19 `los_icu` conflicts. Comparing its unchanged Encounter views with attempt_0001's direct `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` cast isolates 6 start, 3 end, and 9 LOS conflicts as the attempt_0002 parser regression, while the direct-cast baseline still had 10 `icu_intime` and the same 10 derived `los_icu` conflicts. `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_encounter_icu.sql:31-33,44,97-100` casts both source endpoints through `TIMESTAMPTZ`, writes only those transformed values, builds the resource UUID from `stay_id` rather than either time, and does not serialize the selected source `icu.los`; no alternate ICU Encounter field preserves the pre-cast endpoint or source LOS.
