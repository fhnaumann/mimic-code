# FHIR-prober evidence

Read the source carryover, loop contract, AGENTS.md, curated MIMIC_NOTES.md, all existing MIMIC_NOTES.d fragments, the ICU MedicationAdministration ETL, and the authoritative demo Delta with embedded Pathling/Spark. Mapped `inputevents.itemid=221653` to ICU `MedicationAdministration` medication coding system `http://mimic.mit.edu/fhir/mimic/CodeSystem/mimic-medication-icu`, exact code `221653`; 44/44 target resources were identified without fan-out. Mapped `stay_id` through `context.getReferenceKey(Encounter)` to the ICU Encounter identifier value, `rate` and `amount` through Quantity dose/rate values, and effective start/end through both Period and dateTime choice paths, casting strings to `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` before coalescing. `linkorderid` has no FHIR representation and must be a typed NULL; source starttime is unavailable on the rate-null dateTime ETL branch.

The demo mapping had 44/44 natural-key rows and exact times/IDs; Quantity values were served at decimal scale six. Dataset-wide findings were appended to `mimic-iv/concepts_fhir/MIMIC_NOTES.d/dobutamine.md` for missing `linkorderid`, conditional effective encoding, and Quantity decimal scale. Reusable artifact: `mimic-iv/concepts_fhir/carryover/dobutamine/fhir-prober.md`; carryover ledger recorded the stage.
