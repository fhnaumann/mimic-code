# FHIR-prober evidence

The prober used embedded Pathling 9.6.0/Spark 4.0.2 over the authoritative demo Delta and read the canonical SQL, source carryover, curated notes, ETL SQL, and ViewDefinition conventions. It mapped ICU chartevents Observations filtered by `mimic-chartevents-d-items` plus codes `226512`/`224639`, and ICU Encounters filtered by the `encounter-icu` identifier system. Observation encounter references join by opaque equality to Encounter resource keys; `stay_id` is emitted from ICU Encounter `identifier.value` and cast to integer. Effective dateTime and ICU period endpoints are offset-bearing strings requiring direct `TIMESTAMP_NTZ` casts. Quantity aliases materialize as strings, so numeric casting precedes the canonical three-decimal round.

The demo target had 129 admit and 441 daily rows, 570/570 source/FHIR matches, no duplicate target groups, and exact final three-decimal weights. The prober found no target-specific DST shift or omission, but documented the global chartevents omission predicates and possible DST propagation through this concept's window/backfill logic. Reusable mapping was written to `mimic-iv/concepts_fhir/carryover/weight_durations/fhir-prober.md` and recorded. The dataset-wide omission finding was appended to `mimic-iv/concepts_fhir/MIMIC_NOTES.d/weight_durations.md`.
