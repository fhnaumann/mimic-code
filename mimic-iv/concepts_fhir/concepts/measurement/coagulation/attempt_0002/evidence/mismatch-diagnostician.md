The full contested review is a fixable attempt-scoped SQL bug. `concept.sql:33` coalesces two string effective-time projections with a native Spark timestamp (`effective_instant`), causing Spark to promote the expression to an offset-aware timestamp and convert the New York offset into the session timezone before the late TIMESTAMP_NTZ cast. This explains the 14–16 hour shift on every charttime; ordinary FHIR strings retain the oracle wall-clock digits and are recoverable by direct `TRY_CAST(effective_datetime AS TIMESTAMP_NTZ)`.

The fix is to parse the populated `effective_datetime` string directly before aggregation, or cast each string variant separately before any COALESCE; do not coalesce the native timestamp variant. The current 100% conflict is therefore not intrinsic and must not go to the judge. A narrower intrinsic residual may remain for DST-gap timestamps because `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_observation_labevents.sql:15,121` writes through TIMESTAMPTZ and normalizes nonexistent wall times; the duplicate specimen ETL is at `mimic-fhir/sql/fhir_specimen_lab.sql:18,58`, but that cannot explain the present systematic shift. No carryover stage is blamed. A dataset-wide note was appended to `MIMIC_NOTES.d/coagulation.md` documenting that datetime choice variants must be cast before COALESCE.
